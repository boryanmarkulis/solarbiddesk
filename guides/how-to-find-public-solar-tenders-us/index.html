How to find public solar tenders in the US

Answer: Start with the state and local boards where your buyers actually post — NYS Contract Reporter in New York, Texas ESBD, and California PlanetBids agency portals — then overlay SAM.gov for federal notices. Cross-check due dates on the agency page. Or browse SolarBidDesk’s free scored directory for the CA/TX/NY wedge and get the weekly digest.

Why this is hard for EPC desks

School, municipal, water, county and authority solar / storage / EV-charger work rarely lives in one feed. Documents sit on per-agency portals; titles vary (“PPA”, “design-build PV”, “EVSE”, “BESS”); and addenda change due dates. Paid aggregators help, but the official notice still wins for bonding, prevailing wage and submission rules.

Core public boards (launch wedge)

New York — NYS Contract Reporter

Search open ads on nyscr.ny.gov with keywords such as solar, photovoltaic, battery, or EV charging. Many authorities, SUNY campuses and municipalities post here. Full document packs may require a free account.

Texas — ESBD / Texas SmartBuy

Browse txsmartbuy.gov/esbd. The public list supports keyword filters and a CSV export (row caps apply). Good coverage for state agencies, universities and many local members.

California — PlanetBids (and friends)

Hundreds of cities, school districts and water agencies use PlanetBids. Start from the California vendor directory, then open each agency’s bid search. Caltrans also publishes some work via CCOP. Statewide Cal eProcure is a poor httpx scrape target — prefer agency portals.

National overlay — SAM.gov

Use SAM.gov Opportunities (API available with a public key) for federal and some local notices. Filter by state and energy-related NAICS/PSC when useful. SAM does not replace muni/school boards.

A practical weekly method

  1. Pick geography. For most installer desks: California, Texas, New York first.
  2. Run the same keyword set on NYSCR, ESBD, top PlanetBids agencies, and SAM.
  3. Record due date, buyer type, tech, and source URL in one sheet.
  4. Read the official PDF/portal before pricing — check addenda, site walks, bonds, DSA (CA schools), prevailing wage.
  5. Score ruthlessly. Due in 10–28 days with clear solar/storage/EV scope usually beats vague “energy” RFQs.

FAQ

Where are US public solar tenders posted?

There is no single national board for local work. California muni and school notices often appear on PlanetBids agency portals; Texas posts many state and local solicitations on the Electronic State Business Daily (ESBD); New York uses the NYS Contract Reporter (NYSCR). Federal and some local opportunities also appear on SAM.gov.

What keywords should I search for?

Use combinations of solar, photovoltaic, PV, battery, BESS, energy storage, EV charger, EV charging, and electric vehicle charging. Add buyer words such as school, municipal, water district, county, or university when the portal allows.

How do I avoid missing due dates?

Check portals on a fixed cadence, download any CSV exports (ESBD supports CSV), and subscribe to free agency alerts where offered. A weekly scored digest is a practical backup when you cannot watch every portal daily.

Are aggregator sites enough?

Aggregators help discovery but can lag or gate documents. Always open the issuing agency’s official notice for addenda, bonding, prevailing wage, and submission instructions.
