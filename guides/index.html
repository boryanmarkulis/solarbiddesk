All guides

  • How to find public solar tenders in the US: Start with the boards where buyers post (NYS Contract Reporter, Texas ESBD, California PlanetBids portals), then each agency's own portal.
  • Solar RFP template: what public agencies ask for: A public solar RFP usually has eight parts: scope and site data, sizing, contract model, interconnection, prevailing wage and bonding, evaluation criteria, schedule and submission rules.
  • Solar PPA RFPs: how public agencies buy solar: A developer designs, builds, owns and operates the system on agency property and sells the power under a PPA, chosen on price per kWh plus qualifications.
  • California school district solar RFPs, PPAs and Gov Code 4217: Schools buy by formal RFP, PPA or an energy service contract under Government Code 4217.10 to 4217.18 after a public-hearing finding.
  • EV charging RFPs explained: scope, NEVI/CFI funding: Agencies buy Level 2, DC fast charging or turnkey charging-as-a-service, often funded by federal NEVI or CFI money or state programs.
  • Battery storage procurement and supplier prequalification: Covers how agencies buy storage, what supplier prequalification asks for, and an evaluation checklist.
  • How to respond to a solar RFP: Decide bid/no-bid fast, build a compliance matrix, write one section per scoring criterion, price to the contract model, submit early.
  • Solar bid bonds, performance and payment bonds: Bid bond with the bid, then performance and payment bonds before work; federal, CA, TX and NY thresholds.
  • Prevailing wage requirements for public solar projects: State public-works wage laws (CA DIR, TX 2258, NY Labor Law Article 8) plus the federal IRA prevailing wage and apprenticeship rules.
  • Elective pay for solar and storage at public entities: How public owners get 48/48E credits as cash, and the 2025-law solar deadlines (storage exempt).
  • ESPC vs PPA for schools and cities: Agency-owned upgrades paid from guaranteed savings vs developer-owned solar sold per kWh.
  • NEVI RFPs and CFI grants for EV charging: NEVI is state-run formula money restarted under August 2025 guidance; CFI has no new round.
  • Public solar procurement rules by state (CA, TX, NY): Bid thresholds, bonds, wage laws, energy-contract statutes and portals for California, Texas and New York.
