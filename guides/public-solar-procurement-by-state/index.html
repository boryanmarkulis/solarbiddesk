Public Solar Procurement by State: California, Texas and New York

Public solar buying differs by state: who must bid, above what amount, where notices post and which contract models are allowed. California

  • Where bids post: each agency's own portal; hundreds of cities, school districts and water agencies use PlanetBids. There is no single statewide feed for local work.
  • Rules that shape solar bids: school districts must award public projects of $15,000 or more to the lowest responsible bidder, with bid security (PCC 20111); a payment bond is required over $25,000 (Civil Code 9550); prevailing wage and DIR registration apply to public works (DIR).
  • Energy contracts: Gov Code 4217.10 to 4217.18 let agencies sign energy service contracts, including solar, after a public-hearing finding (4217.12).

Texas

  • Where bids post: the statewide Electronic State Business Daily (ESBD on Texas SmartBuy) for state agencies and many local bodies; big cities and utilities also run their own portals.
  • Thresholds: a municipality must use competitive sealed bids or proposals (or another listed method) for contracts over $100,000 (Local Gov. Code 252.021). Performance bonds over $100,000 and payment bonds over $25,000, or $50,000 for cities (Gov. Code 2253).
  • Labor and energy contracts: prevailing wage on public works (Gov. Code 2258); local governments can use energy savings performance contracts under Local Gov. Code ch. 302.

New York

  • Where bids post: the NYS Contract Reporter carries state agency, authority and many local notices; NYSERDA and NYPA also run programs on their own sites.
  • Thresholds: political subdivisions must advertise for sealed bids on public work over $35,000 and purchase contracts over $20,000, and award to the lowest responsible bidder furnishing the required security; purchase contracts may use best value where authorized (General Municipal Law 103).
  • Labor: public work prevailing wage under Labor Law Article 8, enforced by the NYS DOL Bureau of Public Work.

Federal and other states

Federal notices post on SAM.gov; federal construction over $150,000 needs performance and payment bonds (FAR 28.102-1). Other states follow the same pattern: a state board for state agencies, local portals for cities and schools, a bid threshold, bonding and a prevailing wage law. FAQ

How do public agencies buy solar in California?

Through their own portals (many use PlanetBids): low-bid construction contracts with bid security and payment bonds, RFPs for design-build or PPAs, or energy service contracts under Gov Code 4217.10-4217.18 after a public-hearing finding. Prevailing wage and DIR registration apply to public works.

When does a Texas city have to bid a solar project?

Local Government Code 252.021 requires competitive sealed bidding or proposals (or another listed method) for municipal contracts over $100,000. Bond and prevailing wage rules then apply under Government Code chapters 2253 and 2258.

What is the New York bidding threshold for public works?

General Municipal Law 103 requires sealed bids for public work contracts over $35,000 and purchase contracts over $20,000 for political subdivisions, unless an exception applies.

Where are public solar RFPs posted in each state?

California: agency portals such as PlanetBids. Texas: the ESBD on Texas SmartBuy plus city and utility portals. New York: the NYS Contract Reporter plus NYSERDA and NYPA sites. Federal: SAM.gov. SolarBidDesk lists the verified open ones by state.
