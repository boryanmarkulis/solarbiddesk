Prevailing Wage Requirements for Public Solar Projects

Public solar work usually carries prevailing wage twice: state public-works law on the contract, and federal clean energy credit rules if the owner claims the full credit. Price both from the notice before you bid. As of November 26, 2026 we track 7 open verified public bids in California, Texas and New York.

State public-works prevailing wage (CA, TX, NY)

  • California: anyone working on a public works project must be paid prevailing wages as determined by the Department of Industrial Relations, and projects of $30,000 or more must meet DIR apprenticeship requirements (DIR Public Works). Contractors and subcontractors must be registered with DIR to bid on public works (Labor Code 1725.5).
  • Texas: a worker on a public work for the state or a political subdivision must be paid at least the general prevailing rate of per diem wages; maintenance work is excluded. The public body sets rates by local survey or by using U.S. Department of Labor Davis-Bacon rates (Gov. Code ch. 2258).
  • New York: the state's prevailing wage laws are in Articles 8 and 9 of the Labor Law, and Article 8 covers public work. The Department of Labor publishes the wage schedule each July 1, and since December 31, 2025 contractors and subcontractors on Article 8 projects must submit certified payroll electronically (NYS DOL Bureau of Public Work).

The notice names the wage schedule. Add certified payroll time to your bid; it is real overhead.

Federal clean energy credits: prevailing wage and apprenticeship (PWA)

Under the Inflation Reduction Act, meeting prevailing wage and apprenticeship requirements raises the base credit amount five times (IRS PWA page); for the investment credit that is the gap between a 6% and a 30% base rate. The rules cover laborers and mechanics doing construction, alteration or repair of the facility. The IRS lists two exceptions: facilities under 1 megawatt, and facilities that began construction before January 29, 2023. Rates come from Department of Labor wage determinations on SAM.gov, including fringe benefits (DOL: Prevailing Wage and the IRA). The apprenticeship part requires registered apprentices to perform a share of total labor hours (15% for construction beginning in 2024 or later, per the IRS PWA FAQ), plus ratio and participation rules.

Why this matters even when the agency pays no tax: public owners can claim credits through elective pay, and PPA developers claim them directly. Either way, the owner will pass PWA duties and records down to you. See our elective pay guide.

Bid-day checklist for wage compliance

  • Find the wage schedule or determination named in the notice and the date it locks.
  • Map each trade you use (electricians, laborers, operators, roofers) to a classification.
  • Price fringe benefits, overtime and travel at the posted rates.
  • Confirm registration (California DIR) and apprenticeship sponsor contacts before the deadline.
  • Plan certified payroll, subcontractor flow-down clauses and record retention.
  • If the owner plans to claim a federal credit, ask in writing whether PWA applies and who keeps the records.

FAQ

Do public solar projects require prevailing wage?

Usually yes. California, Texas and New York all have public-works prevailing wage laws that cover construction paid with public funds, and the notice names the schedule. Federal clean energy credits add separate prevailing wage and apprenticeship requirements for the full credit amount.

What are the IRA prevailing wage requirements for solar?

To get the increased (5x) credit, laborers and mechanics doing construction, alteration or repair must be paid at least DOL prevailing wages including fringe, and registered apprentices must perform the required share of labor hours. Facilities under 1 MW and facilities that began construction before January 29, 2023 are excepted (IRS).

Is there prevailing wage on public solar projects in California?

Yes. Public works in California require prevailing wages set by DIR, projects of $30,000 or more must meet DIR apprenticeship requirements, and contractors must be registered with DIR to bid. Check DIR for whether a specific project counts as public works.

Does Texas require prevailing wage on public solar work?

Texas Government Code chapter 2258 requires at least the prevailing per diem wage for workers on public works of the state and political subdivisions, excluding maintenance work. The public body sets the rates by survey or by using Davis-Bacon rates.
