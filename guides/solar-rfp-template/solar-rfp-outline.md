# Solar RFP outline (public agencies)

Source: SolarBidDesk, https://solarbiddesk.com/guides/solar-rfp-template/ . Updated 2026-11-23. Checklist of what public solar RFPs usually contain. Not legal advice. Always follow the specific solicitation.

## 1. Scope and site data

What the buyer wants built or bought, and where. Include site addresses or parcels, roof or land type, existing electrical service, load profile or utility bills, available area, and any restrictions (landfill, canopy, historic, easements). Scope should say whether storage or EV charging is bundled.

## 2. System sizing

Target size in kW or MW (DC and AC), annual kWh goal or percent of load offset, storage power and duration if any, and whether the proposer may offer alternatives. Say if size is a ceiling, a floor or a range.

## 3. Ownership and contract model

EPC or design-build (agency owns the system), PPA (developer owns, agency buys energy), or a statutory energy service contract such as California Gov Code §4217.10-4217.18 / an energy savings performance contract (ESPC) under the state's own statute. Include term, rate or price form, escalator, ground or roof lease, and end-of-term options.

## 4. Interconnection

Who applies and pays, the utility and tariff that governs (for example CPUC Rule 21 in California IOU territory; the NY Standardized Interconnection Requirements for DG and storage of 5 MW or less), and whether the price assumes known upgrade costs or leaves them to the proposer.

## 5. Prevailing wage, apprenticeship and bonding

Wage regime that applies (California DIR public works; New York Labor Law Articles 8 and 9), payroll reporting, apprenticeship rules, licensing, and bid, performance and payment bond requirements as stated in the notice.

## 6. Evaluation criteria

Weighted criteria with points: price or energy rate, technical approach and design, experience on comparable public projects, schedule, financing strength (PPA), safety record, local or workforce commitments. Say whether price is scored or used as a tiebreaker.

## 7. Schedule

Release date, pre-proposal meeting or site walk, question deadline, addenda policy, proposal due date and time zone, shortlist or interview dates, award and board approval dates, notice to proceed, target commercial operation date.

## 8. Submission requirements

Where and how to submit (portal or sealed), number of copies, format and page limits, required forms (non-collusion, insurance, references), pricing sheet, exceptions to the contract, and the named contact for questions.

### Questions to answer in each section

- [ ] Scope: sites, roof/land, utility service, load data, bundled storage/EV?
- [ ] Sizing: DC/AC kW, kWh goal, storage kW/kWh, alternates allowed?
- [ ] Ownership: EPC, PPA or energy service contract; term, price form, escalator, lease, end of term?
- [ ] Interconnection: governing tariff, applicant, who pays upgrades?
- [ ] Labor: prevailing wage, apprenticeship, licensing, bonds, insurance?
- [ ] Evaluation: criteria, weights, price treatment?
- [ ] Schedule: questions due, addenda, due date/time zone, award, NTP, COD?
- [ ] Submission: portal or sealed, forms, page limits, contact?
