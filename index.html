FAQ

What is in the free digest?

One email a week with counts by state and tech, plus anonymised themes. Agency names and source links are not included.

What does the score mean?

A 0-100 fit score for an installer or EPC desk, from the due date, the technology, the buyer type and how complete the public package looks. The letter is the band published with that score. It is a screen, not a bid recommendation.

Why is the agency column locked?

The sample is anonymised. Pro shows the agency and the source link for each tender.

Which places are covered?

The 24 October 2026 open set is New York public buyers. Pro is set up for California, Texas and New York.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Monthly Pro cancels in the Stripe customer portal. Yearly Pro and Team are paid once a year.

Do you submit the bids?

No. SolarBidDesk sends the shortlist. Your desk decides what to pursue and submits the bid.
