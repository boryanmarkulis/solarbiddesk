NYSERDA and NYPA storage programs (check these directly)

Most New York storage money moves through state programs rather than one-off municipal bids. New York's goal is 6,000 MW of energy storage by 2030 (PSC order, June 2024), split across residential, commercial/community (retail) and bulk storage. FAQ

Where can I find New York battery energy storage (BESS) RFPs?

NYSERDA's Current Funding Opportunities page and Energy Storage Program pages, NYPA's procurement portal, and the NY Contract Reporter (NYSCR). SolarBidDesk screens NYSCR daily and lists open NY storage notices with direct source links. Today we track 3 open New York storage bids.

Who buys energy storage in New York?

NYSERDA (bulk and retail storage programs and incentives), the New York Power Authority (NYPA), investor-owned utilities through PSC-directed storage procurements, and public site owners such as municipalities, SUNY campuses and school districts.

What is the typical scope of a NY storage RFP?

Depending on the buyer: developer selection or land lease for a storage site, qualification of storage technology or pipeline partners (RFQ), EPC or design-build of a BESS (often paired with solar), incentive programs for residential and commercial storage, and technical assistance.

Does NYSERDA run a bulk energy storage RFP?

NYSERDA runs a Bulk Energy Storage Program under the 2024 PSC order (3,000 MW of new large-scale storage). Check NYSERDA's Bulk Energy Storage Program and Current Funding Opportunities pages for open rounds and deadlines.

Are there commercial battery storage incentives in Albany, NY?

Yes. Commercial projects across New York, including the Albany area, use NYSERDA's Residential and Retail Energy Storage incentives. See the NYSERDA Developers & Contractors page for current terms.
