Where New York agencies post solar bids

Most New York public solar solicitations appear on the NY Contract Reporter (NYSCR), the state's official procurement notice board for agencies, authorities and many municipalities. Some buyers also run their own portals: NYPA procurement (SAP Ariba registration) and NYSERDA current funding opportunities (PONs, RFPs, RFQs). We list a row only when it appears as an open notice on NYSCR and we have confirmed the notice live.

Typical scopes

  • Landfill and brownfield solar (e.g. "Albany GSD Landfill Solar PV Project EPC Services")
  • EPC / design-build for campus, municipal and authority sites
  • Rooftop and carport solar for schools and public buildings
  • Power purchase agreements (PPA) on public land or roofs
  • Program and predevelopment solicitations such as affordable solar and storage

How to use this list

Open the official notice, register on the portal named in it, and check mandatory pre-bid meetings and addenda. Rows marked with a far-future due date are standing program notices, not one-off bids. Today's featured landfill bid: Albany GSD Landfill Solar PV Project EPC Services.

FAQ

Where can I find open New York solar RFPs?

On the NY Contract Reporter (NYSCR) for state agencies, authorities and many municipalities, plus NYPA's procurement portal and NYSERDA's funding opportunities page. SolarBidDesk screens NYSCR daily and lists open NY solar notices with a verified link to the official notice. Today we track 3 open New York solar bids.

What is the difference between NYSCR and agency portals?

NYSCR is the state's central notice board where agencies and many municipalities must advertise larger procurements. Agencies such as NYPA and NYSERDA also run their own portals for registration, Q&A and submission, so use NYSCR to discover the notice and the agency portal to bid.

What is the typical scope of a New York solar RFP?

Landfill or brownfield solar EPC, campus and municipal rooftop or carport systems, PPAs on public property, and program or predevelopment solicitations. Scope, bonding and prevailing-wage requirements are in the official notice.

How long are New York solar RFPs open?

Usually several weeks, often with a pre-bid meeting and written question deadline. Some program notices stay open for years. Always confirm the due date on the official notice.

Do I need to register to bid?

Usually yes, on the portal named in the notice (for example NYPA's Ariba or the NY State Contract Reporter's agency contact process). SolarBidDesk links straight to the notice.
