FAQ

Where can I find open solar RFPs in 2026?

State portals (Texas ESBD, NY Contract Reporter, California agency PlanetBids portals), SAM.gov for federal work, and aggregators. SolarBidDesk lists CA/TX/NY solar bids daily with direct source links and no login. Today we track 2 open solar bids.

Who issues public solar RFPs?

School districts, cities and counties, water/wastewater districts, universities and state authorities, usually for EPC, PPA or energy-services contracts.

What is the difference between a solar RFP, an IFB and an RFQ?

RFP: proposal scored on price and qualifications. IFB: lowest responsive bid wins. RFQ: shortlists qualified firms first.

How long are solar RFPs open?

Typically 3–6 weeks, often with a mandatory pre-bid site walk.

Do I need to register to bid?

Yes, usually on the agency's portal (PlanetBids, ESBD/CMBL, NYSCR). SolarBidDesk links straight to the source.
