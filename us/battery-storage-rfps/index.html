FAQ

What is a BESS RFP?

A solicitation to procure, build or contract battery energy storage capacity or a storage project, from a utility, state agency or public site owner.

Where are NY energy storage RFPs posted?

NYSERDA solicitations, NYPA, and the NY Contract Reporter. SolarBidDesk aggregates NYSCR storage notices.

Who can respond to a public storage RFP?

Developers, EPCs and integrators, often with bonding, prevailing-wage and interconnection experience.
