How to search ESBD yourself

On txsmartbuy.gov/esbd, search keywords (solar, photovoltaic, EV charging, electric vehicle supply equipment, energy storage, energy management) and NIGP class codes for solar, EVSE and electrical work. Many state agency bids require CMBL registration.

FAQ

What is ESBD Texas?

The Electronic State Business Daily on txsmartbuy.gov, where Texas state agencies (and some local entities) post solicitations over $25,000.

How do I find solar bids on ESBD?

Search keywords (solar, photovoltaic, EV charging, energy storage) and NIGP codes, or use SolarBidDesk's filtered daily list.

Do I need CMBL registration?

Many state agency bids require registration on the Centralized Master Bidders List.

Do Texas cities post on ESBD?

Most do not. They use their own portals or co-ops, so check those too.
