How California public agencies buy solar

  • PPA: a developer owns the system on agency land or roofs and sells the power.
  • Design-build / EPC: the agency owns the system.
  • Gov Code §4217 energy-services contracts: award after a public hearing finding that energy savings exceed costs.
  • DSA approval for K-12 school projects.
  • Prevailing wage on public works.

Where California posts bids

PlanetBids portals (many cities and districts), Cal eProcure for state agencies, Caltrans CCOP, and school district bond and procurement pages.

FAQ

Where are California solar RFPs posted?

On each agency's portal (often PlanetBids), Cal eProcure for state agencies, and school district bond/procurement pages.

What is a §4217 energy services contract?

Under CA Government Code §4217.10–.18, a public agency can award an energy project without a traditional low bid after a public hearing finding that energy cost savings exceed costs.

Do California school solar projects need DSA approval?

Yes, K-12 projects need Division of the State Architect approval.

Is prevailing wage required?

Yes for public works, including most PPA-hosted school systems.
