FAQ

Which California EV charging RFPs are open right now?

As of November 25, 2026 we list 1 verified open solicitation: HD-S3203 HARBOR FLEET ZERO EMISSIONS INFRASTRUCTURE (Port of Long Beach, due 2026-12-08). Each is in the table on this page with its official notice link.

Where do California agencies post EV charging bids?

Many California cities, counties, ports, transit and school districts post solicitations on their own PlanetBids vendor portal, which is free to search once you register. We read the open bid lists of the portals we track daily and keep only EV charging notices that pass a field check.

Do I need to register to bid on a California EV charging RFP?

Usually yes: you register as a vendor on the agency's PlanetBids portal, then submit through it. The official notice link in the table opens the agency's own bid page, where the registration and submission rules are stated.

What do these California EV charging bids usually cover?

Scopes range from construction of the electrical infrastructure and underground utilities behind chargers to charger supply, installation, charge-management software and O&M. Read the notice for what is in scope; our table quotes the notice rather than paraphrasing it.

Is every California EV charging bid listed here?

No. We only list notices from the PlanetBids portals we track that match EV charging language and pass a daily field and link check. Agencies on other platforms, and notices that do not name charging in the title, can be missed. Check the agency's portal before you rely on this list.

How often is this list updated?

Daily. A row appears only if it passes a field check against the agency's PlanetBids notice and a live link check, and it drops off when its due date passes.
