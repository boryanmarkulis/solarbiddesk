FAQ

Where do cities post EV charger RFPs?

On their procurement portals (PlanetBids, Bonfire, bidsandtenders), state boards (ESBD, NYSCR) and SAM.gov. SolarBidDesk filters CA/TX/NY daily.

What is usually in an EV charging RFP scope?

Site design, electrical/make-ready, charger supply, install, commissioning, network software, and multi-year O&M.

Are EV charger bids lowest-price or best-value?

Construction ITBs are mostly lowest responsive bid. Turnkey and network contracts are usually best-value RFPs.

What funding programs drive public EV charger bids?

NEVI formula funds, the federal CFI grant program, and state programs.
