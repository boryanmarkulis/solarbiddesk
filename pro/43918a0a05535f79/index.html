How the score works

Each bid gets a 0–100 fit score for solar, storage and EV-charger installers and EPCs, built from five parts:

  • Tech fit: solar PV and EV charging score highest; storage counts; generic battery/UPS work doesn't.
  • Size: larger stated contract values score higher; unknown size gets a neutral middle value.
  • Time to respond: enough runway before the due date scores higher than a bid closing in days.
  • Location: CA, TX and NY public buyers score highest.
  • Barriers: open competitive bids score highest; restricted eligibility or an unusual status (e.g. addendum posted) scores lower.

Past-due, closed and awarded bids are removed. A score ranks fit; always read the official notice before bidding.
